## Brand & Style
The design system positions the platform at the intersection of rigorous academic achievement, next-generation AI intelligence, and creator-economy prosperity. It marries the clarity of modern enterprise software with the luminescent, high-energy atmosphere of frontier AI interfaces. 

Targeted at self-directed students, elite knowledge creators, and global educators, the interface instills confidence, momentum, and technical refinement. The visual language utilizes a "Precision Dark-Slate SaaS" aesthetic: deep slate structural surfaces, razor-sharp hairline borders, luminous glassmorphism badges, and controlled electric atmospheric glows. It avoids gratuitous 3D clutter in favor of high-legibility typography, tactile micro-cards, and high-contrast transactional clarity for global checkout and multi-currency monetization.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on high-contrast chromatic accents grounded by deep slate foundations:

- **Primary (`#6366F1` - Electric Indigo):** The engine of the interface. Powers active states, course progress indicators, interactive CTAs, and key focus rings.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Luminous Emerald):** Symbolizes growth, course completion, earnings, payouts, and Mobile Money/Fintech success states.
- **Tertiary (`#8B5CF6` - Cyber Violet):** Dedicated to the synthetic AI tutor dimension—chat feeds, AI breakdown chips, automated insights, and predictive roadmaps.
- **Neutral Palette (`#0F172A` Slate Base):**
  - Background Canvas: `#090D16` (Deep Obsidian Slate)
  - Surface Tier 1 (Cards, Modules): `#0F172A`
  - Surface Tier 2 (Elevated, Dialogs): `#1E293B`
  - Surface Borders (Hairlines): `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`
  - Elevated Glows: `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`

Role-specific semantic badges:
- **Étudiant:** Tinted slate base with subtle Indigo edge (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`, text `#A5B4FC`).
- **Professeur:** Tinted slate base with Emerald edge (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#6EE7B7`).
- **Admin:** Tinted slate base with Amber edge (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#FCD34D`).

## Typography
The system enforces a crisp triple-font hierarchy designed for technical readability and marketing punch:

- **Display & Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric yet friendly contours deliver modern SaaS energy. Tight letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) creates cohesion across course titles and dashboard metrics.
- **Interface & Prose (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral workhorse engine used across lecture transcripts, AI conversation threads, syllabi, and transactional receipts.
- **System Metrics & Role Badges (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for technical metadata, timestamp offsets in videos, currency outputs (e.g., `XOF`, `USD`, `EUR`), code exercises, and role identifiers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column adaptive fluid grid with strict maximum container widths:
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, dynamic auto margins clamped at `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px edge padding. Dual-pane video/AI drawer transitions to an overlay bottom-sheet or segmented view.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px outer margin. Layout reflows into single-column vertical stacks.

Consistent 4px/8px incremental micro-spacing standardizes padding: inputs and interactive pills adhere strictly to `space-sm` and `space-md` intervals, while course content cards leverage `space-lg` to maintain an airy, luxury-grade hierarchy.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons traditional mud-toned drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Luminance, Edge Lighting, and Kinetic Glass**:

1. **Flat Ground (Tier 0 - `#090D16`):** The structural canvas background.
2. **Interactive Surfaces (Tier 1 - `#0F172A`):** Micro-cards, marketplace cards, course list items. Outlined with a fine `1px` translucent edge `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Floating & Glass Panels (Tier 2 - `#1E293B` @ 80% opacity):** AI tutor side-drawers, sticky video controls, floating audio pills. Rendered using `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and illuminated with a subtle directional top border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
4. **Active/Hero Glow Elevation:** Course thumbnail hover states and active payment targets project a localized neon drop shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`.
5. **Success/Payout Elevation:** Revenue cards and Mobile Money confirmation blocks reflect an ambient green halo: `0 10px 30px -6px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.20)`.

## Shapes
The design system operates on `Level 2 (Rounded)` geometry, creating a tactile, ergonomic feel without veering into overly playful bubble aesthetics:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Checkboxes, code blocks, technical chips, and video preview controls.
- **Medium Radius (0.75rem / 12px):** Inputs, buttons, dropdown popovers, and AI chat bubbles.
- **Large Radius (1rem / 16px):** Course cards, checkout receipt blocks, and creator analytics widgets.
- **Pill (9999px):** Status chips, role indicators (`Étudiant`, `Professeur`), and audio playback handles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Electric CTA):** Solid `#6366F1` background, white text (`Inter` 600), subtle inner border highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover: translates 1px upwards with an Indigo ambient bloom.
- **Secondary Action (Glass Ghost):** Slate `#1E293B` background with a hairline border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`, text `#F8FAFC`.
- **Monetization / Instant Checkout:** Solid Emerald `#10B981` background, black text `#022C22`, high visual priority for Mobile Money ("Payer via Orange/MTN/Wave") and card actions.

### Role Badges & Chips
- Ultra-compact rounded pill tags with a semi-translucent base, crisp 1px borders, uppercase tracked micro-type (`JetBrains Mono` or `Plus Jakarta Sans 700` at 11px).
- **Étudiant:** Text `#818CF8`, Background `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.12)`, Border `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.3)`.
- **Professeur:** Text `#34D399`, Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, Border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.
- **Admin:** Text `#FBBF24`, Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, Border `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.

### Course Marketplace Cards (Micro-Cards)
- Composed of 16px rounded slate containers, 16:9 thumbnail frame with a micro-progress bar overlaid at the bottom.
- Includes instructor avatars with verification checkmarks, dynamic star ratings, price chips with multi-currency toggle badges, and a floating "AI Enriched" glass emblem in the corner.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#0B0F19`, 1px border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.16)`. 
- Focus state triggers an immediate Indigo perimeter border `#6366F1` and a soft external glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)`).
- Mobile Money phone inputs display automatic network identification logos (Wave, Orange, MTN, Moov) inline on the right.

### AI Mentor Chat Drawer
- Distinct from standard card modules: features a gradient perimeter line transitioning from Electric Indigo to Cyber Violet.
- Chat messages feature high-contrast asymmetric styling: Student prompts are right-aligned deep slate pills; AI responses are left-aligned translucent dark glass containers accented with an illuminated tutor avatar and interactive "Ask Follow-up" suggestion chips.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 8px rounded corners for checkboxes, custom ring selectors for radios.
- Unchecked: `#090D16` filled with `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.3)` border.
- Checked: `#6366F1` background with crisp white icon checkmark and glowing border.
